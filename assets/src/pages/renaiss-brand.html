introduction

The idea to create Renaiss came to me from an advertisement about an edible piece of art. I decided that the dish shouldn't look like a copy of Van Gogh's masterpiece, it should bring the sensation. It is my reinterpretation of the Renaissance in modern times, how would it look, feel, taste.

The vision of the brand is to provoke the sensation of specific historical period aestetic in twenty-first century.

The main goal of the project is to showcase my Graphic design skills.

  • Logotype

    This simple and yet impactful design evokes a sense of movement, fluidity, and dynamism, symbolizing the constant evolution of tastes. The logo's clean lines and minimalistic approach reflect the restaurant's commitment to modern design.

  • UI

    Bright and vibrant colors in contrast with white motionless sculptures make the design appealing and noticeable. The color palette and typeface balances modernity and elegance. The sleek and legible nature of MuseoModerno font ensures readability while adding a touch of sophistication to all textual elements associated with the Renaiss brand.

  • design goals

    The design goal for the branding is to create a cohesive and visually appealing identity that effectively communicates the essence of the brand. It aims to establish a strong and recognizable presence, leaving a lasting impression on the target audience.

    The design should align with the brand's values, mission, and target market, while also reflecting its unique personality and positioning. The goal is to create a visual language that resonates with the audience, evokes positive emotions, and establishes a strong brand association.

    Ultimately, the design should enhance brand recognition, build trust, and differentiate the brand from its competitors in a market.
