introduction

The aim of the project was to show Design Thinking Process — structured and methodical approach to problem-solving and design. Design thinking involves empathizing with users, defining their needs and problems, ideating potential solutions, prototyping and testing those solutions, and iterating based on user feedback.

This process helps ensure that the final design solutions are effective, usable, and meet the needs of the users.

  • Strategy

    It is crusial to ask a right questions at the begining of the project:

    • What product or service are we building?
    • Why are we doing it?
    • Who are the users?
    • What value we can provide?
    • How can we measure success?
  • UI kit
    1. Color Palette:

      Electric Blue: A vibrant, eye-catching color used for important elements and highlights.

      Lime: A refreshing, lively color used to add accents and bring attention to specific areas.

    2. Typography:

      MuseoModerno: This typeface is the brand's main font and is used for prominent headings, titles, and logo.

      Mulish: As the supplementary font, Mulish is used for copy text, descriptions, and supporting information. It offers readability and versatility.

    3. Buttons and Interactive Elements:

      Buttons are designed with rounded corners, featuring the electric blue color as the background and white text in Mulish font for clear visibility. Hover and active states of buttons can incorporate subtle color transition effects to provide visual feedback to the user.

    4. Navigation:

      The navigation bar presented with logo, main menu, search and language picker, which user can reach from every page.

    5. Icons:

      Icons are designed to maintain consistency throughout the UI. The icons can be used to represent different allergens in dish description.

    6. Imagery:

      High-quality product images can be incorporated into the digital menu to showcase the offerings. These images can be placed within rounded frames to maintain a cohesive look.

    7. Visual Hierarchy:

      The bold and bright colors, such as electric blue, can be used to draw attention to headlines, special offers, or featured items. Contrasting colors, can be used sparingly to highlight specific information or create visual interest.

      8. Overall, the UI kit for the digital menu focuses on maintaining brand identity through the vibrant colors and contrast images. The MuseoModerno typeface establishes a strong brand presence, while Mulish complements it for easy-to-read copy text. The UI kit ensures consistency in layout, typography, colors, and icons, providing a visually appealing and user-friendly experience.

  • design goals

    The main goal was to create convenient web application for the restaurant with human-centered design and intuitive user flow.
