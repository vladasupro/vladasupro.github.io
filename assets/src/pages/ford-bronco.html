introduction

In 2022, Ford made an exciting announcement that the iconic Bronco off-roader would soon be available to European customers. One year prior, I took part in a web development project for Getaway Bronco(gatewaybronco.com), an American customer specializing in the restoration of Ford Broncos through a frame-off restoration process.

Being deeply involved with the Bronco brand, I asked myself: How should the Bronco's website be designed to captivate and engage European customers?

The challenge was clear: I needed to translate the essence of the Bronco's appeal into a visually compelling and intuitive online experience. It was a task that required careful consideration of design elements, interactive features, and user-friendly navigation, all while maintaining the spirit of adventure that defines the Bronco.

  • Models & Specs Page

    High-quality images and videos, interactive 3D models, and detailed specifications helps potential customers to visualize themselves owning and driving the vehicle.

  • UI

    Simple and minimalistic design considering Ford's brand identity.

  • design goals

    Flexible and reusable blocks should seamlessly adapt to variable levels of information density.
